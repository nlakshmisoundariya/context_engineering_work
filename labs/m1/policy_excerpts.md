# Policy excerpts for probe P-2

Copied verbatim from two current NovaTech policy documents. These are the only clauses supplied for the probe.

## DOC-POL-001 · POL-SUP v2.4 — Support & SLA Policy

4.2 Hotfix eligibility. Premium and Elite customers are eligible for hotfix builds for the current and previous minor version of a supported release line. Standard customers are not eligible for hotfix builds; they receive workarounds and the fix in the next scheduled release.

## DOC-POL-007 · POL-EOL v2.1 — Product Lifecycle & EOL Policy

1.2 This policy is the authoritative source for product-version support eligibility, including which versions receive hotfix builds.

3.1 Hotfix eligibility by version. Hotfix builds are provided only for the current minor version of a supported release line for Premium customers; Elite customers are additionally eligible for the previous minor version of that release line. Standard customers receive no hotfix builds.
